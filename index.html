Organisational Profile

Bridging Health Information & Community Care

Established in 2005 in Zambia, Mwaroky HIV/AIDS Savers works at the intersection of HIV/AIDS awareness, prevention education, and frontline community engagement.

Our mission is to bring reliable health education closer to rural and urban communities—with special focus on adolescents, young people, and vulnerable populations to ensure equal access to prevention choices and dignified care.

Founded in 2005

Over 2 decades of dedicated community health work in Zambia.

Peer Navigators

Continuous capacity building and field-level training.

Our Key Objectives

  • Promote HIV prevention education, including awareness of PrEP and PEP (within 72h).
  • Demystify HIV self-testing kits through live demonstrations and accessible distribution.
  • Engage community leaders, protecting adolescent health and the rights of the girl child.
  • Continuous digital and administrative system enhancement to guarantee transparent resource management.

What We Do

Our Core Areas of Work

Delivering multi-faceted interventions from field outreach to policy advocacy.

PrEP & PEP Education

Providing clear education on HIV prevention choices. Promoting immediate Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) within 72 hours and Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP) access.

Self-Testing Awareness

Supporting practical education, demonstrations, and distribution of oral and blood-based HIV self-testing kits for private at-home testing.

Youth & Girl Child Advocacy

Fostering community dialogue with civic and traditional leaders on crucial issues affecting adolescents, gender equity, and the empowerment of young girls.

Peer Navigator Capacity

Training and developing local field personnel and Peer Navigators with standardized tools to deliver empathetic peer-to-peer counselling.

Cultural & Mobile Outreach

Mobilizing health outreach during major cultural events like the Kuomboka Ceremony and conducting remote rural school campaigns under #MyHealthMyFlex.

Systems & Governance

Constantly strengthening digital record management, budget tracking, activity planning, and monitoring to maintain institutional transparency.
